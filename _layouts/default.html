About

I grew up in Shandong Province in northern China. I received my B.S. degree in Mathematics from the Hua Loo-Keng Honors Class at Beihang University, and my M.S. degree in Computer Science from UC Davis. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at George Washington University.
My interests span mathematics, computer science, and physics. Currently, my research mainly focuses on optimal transport theory and neural networks optimization. One interesting finding of my research is the RW2 geometry in the space of probability distributions, which enables concepts such as angles, inner products, and orthogonal projections to be extended from Euclidean space to the probability distributions space.
